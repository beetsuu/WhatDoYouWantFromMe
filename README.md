This is a game I made during the Goofy Game Jam, so naturally it may be a little silly... maybe even a bit amusing.

The concept is pretty simple: at first, you can clearly see what he is thinking about and press the corresponding button. But keep an eye on his facial expressions, because eventually it becomes harder to tell what exactly he wants from you.

I hope you enjoy my dog's dumb expressions as much as I do.

I’d also like to make an Android version eventually, so yeah—
